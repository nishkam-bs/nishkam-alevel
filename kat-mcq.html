KAT multiple-choice quiz

Units 3.1 to 3.8, 80 questions. Do the quiz on paper using the booklet, then hand in your answer sheet here.

Sign in first

Sign in on the home page with your username and password, then come back to this page to hand in your answer sheet.

1. Take a photo of your answer sheet

  1. Lay the sheet flat in good light. Hold the phone straight above it so the whole sheet fills the photo.
  2. Make sure all 80 boxes and your name are in the picture and the letters are sharp.
  3. If you can't fit it in one photo, take two (top half and bottom half).

2. Check your answers

These are the letters read from your photo. Check every one against your sheet and change any that are wrong. Boxes in orange were hard to read. Boxes in red were read as blank. Your teacher can see your photo and any letters you change.

You can only hand in once, so check carefully first.
