Why this matters

Across the 2026 exams, only 20% of students got the ARR question fully correct. Many could not reverse a percentage increase, and multi-step calculations defeated a large proportion. The examiner's message is clear: students can recall formulas but struggle when they need to rearrange a formula or work through several steps with unfamiliar data.

Questions marked REARRANGED require you to work the formula backwards — these are the questions that separate the top grades.
