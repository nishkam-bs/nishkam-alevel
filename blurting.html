Blurting

Blurting means writing down everything you can remember about a topic, without notes, then checking what you missed. It is one of the quickest ways to find the gaps in your knowledge. Everything on this page covers the whole specification.

How to blurt

  1. Pick one topic

    Keep it small - one topic, not a whole unit.

  2. Blurt

    Notes away. Write everything you can remember in five or ten minutes: terms, lists, formulas, models.

  3. Check

    Compare with the blurt sheet. Add what you missed in a different colour.

  4. Revise the gaps

    Only the gaps. Don't reread what you already know.

  5. Blurt it again

    Same topic a few days later. The gaps should shrink each time.
