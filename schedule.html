My schedule

Each week you have written answers to past paper questions (four each week, and nothing in the KAT weeks), a Paper 2 data drill, a Paper 3 case drill, 20 multiple-choice questions and 10 calculations. A course is due every few weeks. Where it says "Choose one", answer one of the questions given. Work is due by Wednesday at 11:59pm each week. Anything not done by then means a detention on Friday after school.

How it works

Click Start to open the question in the right marking tool. Write your answer (or photograph a handwritten one) and get it marked. It is ticked off here as soon as it is marked. For Paper 2 and Paper 3 questions you need the paper or case study, which your teacher will give you. The Paper 2 and Paper 3 drills open the case study on screen; you pick answers by clicking, and only type the numbers you work out. The multiple-choice questions and calculations are done on the site: click an answer and you see the explanation straight away. Your first answer is the one that counts, and they are ticked off once you have answered them all. Courses are ticked off when you have passed every stage.
