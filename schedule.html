My schedule

Your homework, week by week. Everything is due by Wednesday at 11:59pm. Anything not done by then means a detention on Friday after school.

How it works

  • Written answers: click Start to open the question in the right marking tool. Type your answer or upload photos of a handwritten one, then get it marked. For Paper 2 and Paper 3 questions you need the paper or insert, which your teacher will give you.
  • Drills: the case study opens on screen. You choose answers by clicking and only type the numbers you work out.
  • Multiple-choice questions and calculations: done on the site. Your first answer is the one that counts.
  • Everything is ticked off here automatically once it is done or marked.
